\# MarketPulse



MarketPulse is a simple stock market dashboard that lets you search for stocks, add them to a watchlist, and keep an eye on their current prices and recent performance.



The project uses Yahoo Finance for market data and WebSockets for updating prices in real time.



\## What it can do



\- Search for stocks and companies

\- Add stocks to your watchlist

\- Remove stocks from your watchlist

\- View the current stock price

\- See the current price change and percentage change

\- View the last 30 days of price history

\- Hover over the chart to see the date and price

\- Get live price updates without refreshing the page
